# Framework Compass — 61 Defined (no guessing)

Use for Compass table suggestions. Never suggest a framework already discussed in main response. Pick from different categories. Each entry = what it is + when to suggest. Previously undefined now resolved with sources.

## Learning & Teaching 📚
- **Socratic Method:** probe with questions to expose assumptions; suggest when user needs to discover answer, not be told.
- **Feynman Technique:** explain simply as if teaching; suggest when understanding is shaky — rewrite in plain words, find gaps.
- **Bloom's Taxonomy:** Remember→Understand→Apply→Analyze→Evaluate→Create; suggest to set depth target explicitly.
- **5E Model:** Engage→Explore→Explain→Elaborate→Evaluate; suggest for lesson/activity sequences.
- **Kolb's Learning Cycle:** Experience→Reflect→Conceptualize→Experiment; suggest for learning from doing + transfer (pairs with DRIVER Reflect).
- **Gradual Release:** I do → We do → You do; suggest when scaffolding to independence.

## Problem-Solving 🔧
- **STAR Method:** Situation→Task→Action→Result; suggest for behavioral evidence (also Phase-1 scaffold).
- **IDEAL Framework:** Identify→Define→Explore→Act→Look back; suggest for tidy single-loop fixes.
- **PDCA Cycle:** Plan→Do→Check→Act; suggest for iterative improvement with check gate.
- **Root Cause Analysis (5 Whys):** ask why to mechanism; suggest when symptom-fixing loops.
- **GROW Model:** Goal→Reality→Options→Will; suggest for coaching with upfront goal (vs CLEAR-Coaching review emphasis).
- **OODA Loop:** Observe→Orient→Decide→Act; suggest for fast adaptive situations.

## Analysis 📊
- **MECE Framework:** Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive; suggest to audit buckets for overlap/gaps.
- **Porter's Five Forces:** suppliers/buyers/entrants/subs/rivalry; suggest for industry pressure map.
- **PESTLE Analysis:** Political/Economic/Social/Tech/Legal/Env; suggest for macro scan.
- **McKinsey 7S:** Strategy/Structure/Systems/Shared values/Style/Staff/Skills; suggest for org alignment check.
- **Ishikawa Diagram:** fishbone causes; suggest to group causes before picking fix.
- **SWOT with TOWS:** Strengths/Weaknesses/Opportunities/Threats → match to actions; suggest to turn audit into moves.

## Creative & Innovation 💡
- **Design Thinking:** Empathize→Define→Ideate→Prototype→Test; suggest for human-centered builds.
- **Double Diamond:** Discover→Define→Develop→Deliver (diverge/converge x2); suggest to stop premature convergence.
- **SCAMPER:** Substitute/Combine/Adapt/Modify/Put to other use/Eliminate/Reverse; suggest for remixing existing thing.
- **Six Thinking Hats:** White/Red/Black/Yellow/Green/Blue lenses; suggest to separate facts/feelings/risks/ideas.
- **Disney Strategy:** Dreamer→Realist→Spoiler rooms; suggest to protect wild ideas before critique.
- **DRIVER Framework [RESOLVED — edu]:** Define&Discover→Represent→Implement→Validate→Evolve→Reflect (Zhang Purdue AI Finance). Suggest for technical learning builds with AI (code first, validate always). NOT World Bank crash DB. See `reasoning-workflows.md`.

## Decision Making ⚖️
- **Cynefin Framework:** Simple/Complicated/Complex/Chaotic/Disorder; suggest to match method to domain (best practice vs probe-sense-respond).
- **Eisenhower Matrix:** Urgent/Important sort; suggest for prioritization + saying no.
- **Decision Matrix:** weighted criteria score; suggest for multi-option trade-offs.
- **RAPID Decision Making:** Recommend/Agree/Perform/Input/Decide roles; suggest when ownership unclear.
- **Vroom-Yetton:** decide how to decide (autocratic→group); suggest for participation level.
- **Recognition-Primed:** experts pattern-match + mental-simulate; suggest for time-pressured expert calls.

## Strategic Planning 🎯
- **OKRs:** Objective + measurable Key Results; suggest to make Middle/STRETCH concrete.
- **Balanced Scorecard:** Financial/Customer/Process/Learning; suggest to avoid single-metric myopia.
- **Theory of Change:** inputs→activities→outputs→outcomes→impact + assumptions; suggest to surface causal bets.
- **Impact Mapping:** Goal→Actors→Impacts→Deliverables; suggest to kill output without impact.
- **Strategy Map:** cause-effect objective visual; suggest to communicate plan on one page.
- **Hoshin Kanri:** catchball deploy, align top→frontline; suggest for org-wide execution.

## Process Improvement ⚙️
- **Six Sigma DMAIC:** Define→Measure→Analyze→Improve→Control; suggest for defect/variation reduction.
- **Lean A3:** one-page problem→analysis→countermeasure→follow-up; suggest for concise proposals.
- **Kaizen Framework:** small continuous improvements; suggest for habits/systems that compound.
- **Theory of Constraints:** find bottleneck → exploit → subordinate → elevate; suggest when throughput stuck.
- **Process Reengineering:** radical redesign; suggest when tweaks plateau.
- **Value Stream Mapping:** map flow, mark waste; suggest to see end-to-end before fixing.

- **7Cs of Communication:** Clear/Concise/Concrete/Correct/Coherent/Complete/Courteous; suggest as message checklist.
- **Monroe's Sequence:** Attention→Need→Satisfaction→Visualization→Action; suggest for persuasive speeches/proposals.

## Research & Investigation 🔍
- **Scientific Method:** hypothesis→test→analyze→replicate; suggest for causal claims.
- **Grounded Theory:** code→categories→emergent theory; suggest when theory must come from data.
- **Action Research:** plan→act→observe→reflect with participants; suggest for practice improvement.
- **PICO Framework:** Population→Intervention→Comparison→Outcome; suggest for clinical/evidence questions.
- **Systematic Review:** protocol→search→appraise→synthesize; suggest for literature rigor.
- **Evidence-Based Practice:** best evidence + expertise + values; suggest for applied decisions.

## Project Management 📋
- **Agile Scrum:** backlog→sprint→standup→review→retro; suggest for iterative delivery.
- **Waterfall Method:** sequential phases + gates; suggest when requirements stable + compliance heavy.
- **PRINCE2:** principles/themes/processes, stage gates + business case; suggest for governed programs.
- **Critical Path Method:** longest chain sets duration; suggest to find what actually drives date.
- **Kanban System:** visualize→limit WIP→pull; suggest to unclog flow.
- **Lean Project Management:** maximize value, kill waste; suggest to simplify delivery.
- **TRIZ:** inventive principles resolve contradictions; suggest for engineering trade-offs (bonus #61).
